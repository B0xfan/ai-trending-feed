# AI Trending feed

RSS feed of repos from the daily AI Trending report: one item per repo, with
a paragraph description, its star count (snapshot from the day it was added),
a practical-use note, and the link to the repo.

Subscribe: https://b0xfan.github.io/ai-trending-feed/feed.xml

Updated every morning after the daily report. Items are snapshots; star counts
on older entries are not refreshed.
